Organize videos

In Tamareel, uploaded video files are saved as videos. Use series and playlists to make your library easier to browse.

Where videos are placed

An uploaded video first appears in Videos. You can keep a video directly in Videos or place it in a Series. Each video is placed in one of those locations.

For example, keep standalone videos in Videos and group a continuing course or travel log in a Series.

Use series like folders

Use a series to group videos on the same topic. You can add sections within a series to organize its videos in more detail.

For example, create a Cooking Course series, then add sections such as Basics, Appetizers, and Main Dishes to organize the videos.

Playlists are collections of links

A playlist does not create or duplicate a video file. It collects links to videos that are already in your library, in the order you choose.

You can add the same video to several playlists, such as Watch This Weekend, Favorites, and For Beginners, while there is still only one copy of the video.

Which one to use

Use Videos or Series to decide where a video is stored. Use Playlists to collect videos by theme or viewing order.
