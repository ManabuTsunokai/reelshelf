EDITING

About video editing

Tamareel currently lets you cut unwanted portions from one source video.

What you can do

In the editor, select ranges of a video and choose which portions to keep or remove. Currently, cut editing for one source video is supported.

Your source video remains unchanged

Editing never changes the source video file. When you save your edits, a separate edited video is created. This lets you create a shortened version or a video containing only the scenes you want, while keeping the source video.

Organize edited videos just like other videos

Edited videos work like other videos in your library. You can keep them in Videos, put them in a Series, or add them to a Playlist.

Editing does not change storage usage

An edited video does not save a new video file. It records which times from the source video to play. As a result, creating a cut edit does not increase or decrease storage usage; it continues to use the source video's storage.

You cannot delete a source video in use

An edited video uses timing information from its source video during playback. Therefore, you cannot delete a source video while an edited video uses it. To delete the source video, first delete the edited video that uses it or edit that video so it no longer uses the source.
