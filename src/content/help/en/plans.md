## Introductory period

Your introductory access starts when your email address is verified.

| Item | Included |
| --- | --- |
| Uploads | 14 days |
| Playback | 30 days |
| Storage | 10 GiB |
| Data retention | 60 days |

> After the upload period, you cannot add new videos. After the playback period, videos cannot be played. After the retention period, videos, series, playlists, and other data in the library are deleted. Always keep your own copy of important original videos.

## Paid plans

Paid plans are billed monthly and include the storage allowance for your selected plan.

| Plan | Monthly price | Storage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $3.75 | 100 GiB |
| Standard | $17.57 | 500 GiB |
| Plus | $31.25 | 1 TiB |
| Premium | $60.00 | 2 TiB |

> After signing in, use Billing settings to review payment method, plan changes, and cancellation.

## Using a paid plan

Once your paid plan becomes active, your plan's storage allowance applies instead of the introductory upload and playback deadlines. After cancellation, you can continue using the service through the end of the paid period. See the [Terms of Use](/en/terms/) for data retention after a subscription ends.
