## Videos shows all your videos

The Videos page shows all your videos, except for those placed in the Archive as described below.

## Group related videos into a series

Use a series to group videos on the same topic or videos that belong together. Each video can belong to only one series; the same video cannot belong to several series.

Adding a video to a series does not remove it from the Videos page. You can also add sections within a series to organize its videos in more detail.

For example, create a Cooking Course series, then add sections such as Basics, Appetizers, and Main Dishes to organize the videos.

## Playlists are collections of links

A playlist arranges videos for a specific purpose.

You can add the same video to several playlists, such as Watch This Weekend, Favorites, and For Beginners, while there is still only one copy of the video.

A video's playlist memberships are independent of its series, so one video can be included in several playlists.

## Create a cut-edited video

You can select the parts you need from a video and create a cut-edited version. The edited video is treated as a single video, so you can add it to a series or playlist just like any other video.

An edited video references its source video during playback. For this reason, the source video cannot be deleted while the edited video exists.

If you do not need the source video to appear in your regular Videos list, archive it instead of deleting it. This keeps the list tidy while leaving the edited video available.

## Keep the regular list tidy with the archive

Use the archive for videos that you want to keep without showing them in the regular Videos list. Archived videos are retained and can be restored whenever you need them.

For example, you can archive source videos used by edited videos, or other footage that you do not regularly revisit, to keep the regular list tidy.

## Which one to use

- **Videos:** View every video that has not been archived.
- **Series:** Organize related videos into one group per video.
- **Playlists:** Add the same video to multiple collections based on purpose or viewing order.
- **Edited videos:** Organize and play an edited video as a single video while it references its source.
- **Archive:** Keep a video without showing it in the regular Videos list.
